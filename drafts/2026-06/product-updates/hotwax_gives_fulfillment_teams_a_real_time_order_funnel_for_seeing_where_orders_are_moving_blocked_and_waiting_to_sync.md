### Fulfillment health as an operating dashboard

Fulfillment teams need to know where work is building up: open orders, brokering, picking, packing, rejected work, unfillable orders, and held tasks. A report is not enough if the operator still has to search for the queue behind the number.

### What changed

Order Funnel brings product-store and facility-level fulfillment metrics into Order Manager. The supporting services return facility filters, oldest-order indicators, open order counts, unfillable metrics, progress totals, and rejected-order views.

### How the workflow operates

The dashboard connects each metric to the work queue or facility context behind it. Teams can move from a daily health check to the exact facility, queue, or exception category that needs review.

### Operational impact

Fulfillment leadership gets a faster way to spot stuck work, and operators get a more direct path from metric to action.
